01 / musify running

Musify

A self-hosted YouTube Music clone — your library, your server.

Backend is FastAPI with custom Innertube scrapers for search and streaming; frontend is a single hand-written HTML/CSS/JS file, no framework. Most of the work has been under the hood: fixing route-ordering bugs, closing a streaming timeout gap, rewriting a broken playback queue, and cutting the app’s memory footprint down dramatically.

memory: ~5 MB peak → ~500 KB steady state

About

I like building things that actually run on your machine, instead of living in a demo video or depending on someone else’s subscription.
